Your Google data

Your mail stays yours.

Mach talks to Google and to nobody else. No Mach server, no analytics, no ads, no AI training.

  • Reads and changes your mailTo show it, and to do what you tell it: send, archive, star, snooze.gmail.modify
  • Reads contact picturesAddresses and pictures only, to show a sender's face.contacts.readonly · contacts.other.readonly · userinfo.profile
  • Keeps it on your deviceYour mail and your sign-in never leave your Mac or iPhone.

Limited Use. Mach's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
